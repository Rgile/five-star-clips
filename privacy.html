Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

This policy covers the Only in Leonida app (shown as "Only in Leonida" on Google's sign-in screen; earlier called "GTA Clips" and "Five Star Clips") and this website.

Who uses the app

The app is a private tool. The only person who signs in to it is the owner of the Only in Leonida YouTube channel. It is not available to the public, and it does not ask viewers, streamers or anyone else to sign in.

What the app accesses

The app uses YouTube API Services with one permission, youtube.upload. It uses that permission only to upload videos to the channel owner's own YouTube channel and to set each video's title, description, tags and visibility.

The app does not read, collect or store:

  • information about people who watch the videos
  • comments, subscriber lists or analytics
  • any Google account data other than the sign-in token described below

What is stored

When the channel owner signs in, Google issues an access token and a refresh token for the owner's account. The refresh token is stored as an encrypted secret in the private code repository that runs the uploads. It is used only to upload videos to that one channel.

The app also keeps a list of the public Twitch clips it has already posted (clip ID, streamer name, title and view count) so the same clip is not posted twice.

Sharing

No personal data is sold or shared with third parties. Videos are sent to YouTube for the purpose of publishing them on the channel.

Removing access

The channel owner can revoke the app's access at any time from Google's security settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access makes the stored token useless; the token can also be deleted from the repository's secrets.

This website

This site is a static page. It sets no cookies and has no sign-in, forms or analytics. The company that hosts the site may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy policy.

YouTube and Google terms

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Clips and takedowns

Videos on the channel are short excerpts of public Twitch streams and credit the streamer. If you are a streamer and want a clip removed, email the address below and it will be taken down.
